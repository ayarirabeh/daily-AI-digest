01Why now

AI has moved from experiment to expectation.

Every leadership team is asking the same question: where is our AI value? Capital is committed, pilots are running, and the pressure to show a return is mounting.

02The gap

Most AI investment never reaches the P&L.

The failure is rarely the model. It's the absence of a strategy with owners, an operating model that assigns real decision rights to a machine, and governance strong enough to survive a regulator, a board, or a bad quarter.

Enterprises don't have an AI problem. They have a decision problem — and it belongs at the top of the house, not in a lab.
