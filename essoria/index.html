  • Executive & board education — briefings, masterclasses, simulations

Transformation.

How the enterprise works when software can decide

Intelligence changes who decides, who executes and who is accountable. We redesign the operating model around human–agent collaboration — decision rights, controls, org structure — and run the transformation office that makes it hold.

  • Operating model redesign — roles, decision rights, escalation
  • Transformation office and programme leadership
  • Workforce transition and executive enablement
  • Value realisation — measured in the P&L, not in pilots

Governance.

The licence to operate at scale — and to endure

Intelligent systems earn the right to run the business, or they don't run it for long. We build the responsible-AI governance, controls and accountability that let AI scale — and keep it defensible in front of a regulator, a board and a decade.

  • AI governance operating model — decision rights, controls, assurance
  • EU AI Act and regulatory readiness, turned into advantage
  • Risk, security and model assurance for agentic systems
  • Sustainable AI: cost, energy and footprint under control
  • Durability: systems and capability that outlive the technology cycle

Intelligent systems that generate tangible value

Strategy earns its keep in production. We design, build and industrialise the AI solutions, agents and tooling that run the business — value and impact by design, not proof-of-concepts that prove nothing.

Who we are

Advisers for high-stakes decisions.

Essor — French, noun: a rising, a taking of flight. IA — intelligence artificielle. Our name is our thesis: the rise of the enterprise through intelligence.

EssorIA was founded on a simple observation: enterprises are being sold AI everywhere, and advised on it almost nowhere. Between the technology vendors and the generalist consultancies sits a gap — independent, senior, accountable advice on the decade's defining transformation.

We work in bespoke teams that combine executive strategy, enterprise architecture and hands-on AI engineering. We connect strategic thinking with facts, figures and working systems.

Our clients are CEOs, boards and investors facing decisions that cannot be undone cheaply. They come to us when the stakes are high and the answers are not obvious.

EssorIA is founder-led. Every client is advised by the people whose name is on the firm — with reputations at stake, not utilisation targets.
